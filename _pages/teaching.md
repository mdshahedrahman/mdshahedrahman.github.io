## Graduate Teaching Assistant

**Department of Computer Science and Engineering, The University of Texas at Arlington · 2023–present**

I support laboratory instruction, technical coursework, student consultations, and course assessments across undergraduate and graduate computer science courses.

## Courses supported

- **CSE 6392 — Special Topics in AI Supply Chain (Graduate):** AI supply-chain security, model dependencies, and provenance analysis.
- **CSE 4380 / CSE 5380 — Information Security (Undergraduate and Graduate):** Laboratory instruction and course assessments; teaching assistant across three semesters.
- **CSE 5307 — Programming Language Concepts (Graduate):** Technical coursework and student support.
- **CSE 2312 — Computer Organization and Assembly Language Programming (Undergraduate):** Computer architecture and low-level programming.
- **CSE 1310 — Introduction to Computer & Programming in C (Undergraduate):** C programming, debugging, and problem-solving exercises.
- **CSE 1106 — Introduction to Computer Science & Engineering (Undergraduate):** Computational thinking and foundational computer science concepts.

## Mentoring and recognition

I provide laboratory-based mentoring and individualized support for undergraduate students, including programming guidance, problem-solving assistance, and assessment preparation.

In **February 2025**, I was named **Teaching Assistant of the Month** in UTA’s Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

## Teaching interests

I am prepared to teach cybersecurity and information assurance, AI and machine learning, programming languages and computer systems, and foundational computer science. My CV provides the full list of teaching interests.
